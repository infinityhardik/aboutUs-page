Use 100% BWP marine plywood to IS 710 for base cabinets, the sink unit and anything that will see water or steam. MR grade plywood is fine for dry wardrobes, beds and TV units. For carcasses 18mm is the working thickness, and 6mm is enough for back panels.

BWP (Boiling Water Proof) is the higher grade. BWP plywood is bonded to withstand prolonged exposure to water and is the grade for kitchens and bathrooms, while BWR (Boiling Water Resistant) suits areas with only mild humidity. We supply BWP marine plywood and BWP flush doors for wet areas.

MDF (Medium Density Fibreboard) is made from wood fibres bonded with resin under heat and pressure. Unlike plywood it has a smooth, uniform surface with no grain or knots, which makes it ideal for painting, routing and precision shutters, while plywood is stronger for load-bearing carcasses. Motherwood grades run, from entry to top: Interior, Exterior, Super HD+WR (HDHMR) and TurBo+. Compare them on the Motherwood MDF page.

HDHMR stands for High Density High Moisture Resistant board, a denser, more moisture-resistant category of fibreboard. Motherwood's Super HD+WR is an HDHMR-grade board, stocked in 3, 5.5, 7.5, 11, 16.75, 18 and 25mm (8x4 ft), and suits kitchen shutters, bathroom vanities and humid interiors. See HDHMR boards for how it compares with plywood.

TurBo+ is Motherwood's highest-density MDF, stocked in 3, 5.5, 8, 12 and 18mm. Boilo refers to boiling-water resistance: a Boilo board is made to survive boiling-water exposure, which is why TurBo+ is used for CNC-profiled shutters and demanding, humid positions.

Transform Your Interiors with Premium Sunmica & Laminates

Laminates, commonly known in India as Sunmica, are the most popular surfacing material for furniture, wall panels, and cabinets. They are created by fusing multiple layers of kraft paper with phenolic resin under high pressure and temperature, topped with a decorative paper layer that defines the design.

🔍 Buying Guide: Choosing the Right Laminate for Your Project

Not all laminates are the same. Depending on where you plan to use them, the choice of thickness and finish varies:

  • For Kitchens: Always choose 1.0mm thick laminates. Anti-fingerprint matte or acrylic-finish glossy laminates are best as they are easy to clean from oil splashes.
  • For Wardrobes: 1.0mm decorative laminate on the outside and 0.8mm liner laminate on the inside ensures a professional finish and prevents moisture absorption from the back.
  • For Main Doors: Use 1.25mm or 1.5mm thick textured laminates or UV-resistant exterior grade laminates to handle high traffic and light exposure.
  • Adhesive Tip: For a bubble-free finish on quality plywood, always use branded heat-resistant adhesives and ensure proper pressing.

The standard size for decorative laminate sheets in India is 8 feet by 4 feet (2440mm x 1220mm). Some brands also offer specialty sizes like 7x3 feet for doors or oversized sheets for large office tables, but 8x4 is the universal standard stocked by all dealers.

Marllex plywood from our Rajkot stock

Marllex (also written Marlex) is the one plywood brand we stock in both bonding grades, so you can buy the same brand for the dry and wet parts of a job:

  • Marllex MR (semi-waterproof): Gurjan face, red-and-white alternate core, for wardrobes, beds and dry-room furniture. Also in an 18mm 15-ply XL construction.
  • Marllex PF, 100% BWP: phenol-formaldehyde bonded, boiling-water-proof, Gurjan face on a red-and-red hardwood core, for kitchen sink bases, bathroom vanities and anywhere the board will get wet.

MR and PF are different boards. Name the grade when you order, because the same thickness exists in both.

📐 MR or PF: which Marllex do you need?

Use PF (100% BWP) wherever water reaches the board: the sink base, the cabinet under a water purifier, bathroom vanities, and utility and wash areas. Use MR for bedrooms, living rooms and overhead kitchen units, where it does the same job for less. Many kitchens use both: PF for the base units and MR for the overheads.

Marllex plywood carries Glue Line Protection (GLP), so the treatment runs through every glue line, not just the face. Borer protection is guaranteed, and anti-termite treatment is available on request.

18mm: carcass and shutters

Wardrobe and cabinet sides, tops, bottoms and doors. It is the thickness that holds hinges and minifix fittings properly.

12mm: shelves and drawers

Drawer sides, internal dividers and short-span shelves. For shelves wider than about 2.5 ft, step up to 18mm.

6mm and 9mm: backs and panels

Wardrobe and cabinet backs, panelling, ceiling work and infill panels, where the board is fixed to a frame.

Sheet size saves waste

If the part is under 6 or 7 ft, a 6×3 or 7×3 sheet often cuts with less offcut than an 8×4. Send us your cutting list and we will suggest sizes.

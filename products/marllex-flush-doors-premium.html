Every Marllex flush door we stock has a Gurjan face on a solid pinewood core and is A grade. There are two bonding grades, and they are not stocked in the same sizes:

  • 25mm A grade MR (semi-waterproof): for internal room doors in dry positions.

📐 Choosing thickness and grade

25mm MR is the economical choice for bedroom and internal doors with standard hinges and locks. 30mm BWP suits bathrooms and any door that will see water or steam. 35mm BWP is the heavier door, for main entrances, taller openings and doors that carry heavier locksets or closers.

Whatever the grade, seal all six faces of the door, including the top and bottom edges and every edge you trim on site, with paint, polish or laminate before or straight after hanging. An unsealed bottom edge is how most doors take on water.

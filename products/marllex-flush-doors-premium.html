📘 Comprehensive Product Overview

Marllex Premium Flush Doors are constructed using a high-density, solid pine wood core and framed with hardwood side-lippings. Every single door is manufactured in compliance with IS:2202 specifications. They are bonded using BWP (Boiling Water Proof) phenol formaldehyde synthetic resin under extreme heat and pressure, creating an inseparable bond that is highly resistant to warping, twisting, or bending.

📐 Technical Specifications & Standards

The doors feature lock-area reinforcement blocks on both sides, ensuring secure and easy installation of handles, mortise locks, and hinges. Their solid-core structure provides outstanding acoustic insulation (sound dampening) and excellent thermal efficiency for residential and commercial rooms.

📏 Marllex Flush Doors in the Rajkot Range

Marllex premium flush doors are stocked in Rajkot at 25mm, 30mm and 35mm, built as a Gurjan face on a solid pinewood batten core, in two grades: A grade MR (semi-waterproof) for dry internal openings, and A grade PF, 100% BWP — phenol-formaldehyde bonded, boiling water proof — for bathrooms, exterior entries and high-moisture kitchen zones. Ask for the grade by name when you order; both are held in the same sizes.

📐 Standard Door Sizes Stocked

Doors are held in seven heights — 72, 75, 78, 81, 84, 90 and 96 inches — against nine widths in 3-inch steps from 24 to 48 inches. That covers the standard internal room opening at 78x30 and 78x33, the main door at 81x36 and 84x36, and the taller and wider openings that usually get made to order elsewhere.

Thickness is chosen by where the door hangs and what hardware it carries. 25mm and 30mm are typical internal room doors; 35mm suits main doors, taller openings and doors carrying heavier locksets or closers. Every cut edge and the bottom rail should be sealed before hanging, whatever the grade. The flush door range page compares the door types side by side.

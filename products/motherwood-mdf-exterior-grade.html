☀️ What is Motherwood Exterior Grade MDF — 2nd Tier Outdoor MDF?

Motherwood Exterior Grade MDF is the 2nd tier in the MDF quality hierarchy — better than Interior Grade MDF but below Super HD+WR (HDHMR) and TerBo+ (the highest). The correct grade ladder from lowest to highest: Interior Grade → Exterior Grade → Super HD+WR (HDHMR) → TerBo+ (BEST).

Exterior Grade MDF is engineered with special exterior-grade resins for outdoor and semi-outdoor applications — resistant to rain, humidity, and temperature fluctuations. It sits below Super HD+WR and the Boilo grade — the trade name for the highest-density MDF, which Motherwood makes as TerBo+. It is the ideal choice for outdoor furniture, garden structures, exterior cladding panels, and any application exposed to the elements where Interior Grade MDF would not suffice.

What “Exterior Grade” Does and Does Not Mean

Exterior grade suits semi-outdoor and humid positions such as covered ceilings, balcony soffits and shop fit-outs. It is not a substitute for marine plywood in a position that takes direct rain or standing water.

✨ Simson Doors and Boards for Dry Interiors

Simson is one of the brands Mahadev Traders stocks for flush doors and block board. Both lines are MR (semi-waterproof) grade, built on a pinewood core with a Gurjan face, which makes them a dependable, cost-effective choice for bedrooms, living rooms, offices and other dry positions in homes and projects across Gujarat.

Each door and board is sold either plain or under the Simson mark. For bathrooms, kitchen sinks and anywhere the door or carcass will meet water, choose a boiling-water-proof product instead — the grade matters more than the brand.

📏 Choosing a Simson Door or Board

Measure the opening, not the old door. Give us the frame's clear height and width and we will match the nearest stock size; if the opening sits between sizes, the next size up is trimmed on site.

Match the grade to the room. Simson doors and boards are MR grade: right for bedrooms, living areas, offices and wardrobes, wrong for bathrooms and sink units. For those positions see our waterproof bathroom doors.

Compare with the rest of the range. The flush door range and block board range pages set Simson alongside Marllex and the other cores we stock.

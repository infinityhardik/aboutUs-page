📘 Comprehensive Product Overview

Simson Plywood is a popular, cost-effective commercial grade plywood that is widely utilized by developers, builders, and packaging industries across Rajkot and Saurashtra. It is manufactured from selected plantation timbers and bonded with urea-formaldehyde synthetic resin, offering a reliable surface and structural stability for cost-sensitive modular furniture.

📐 Technical Specifications & Standards

Featuring good screw-holding capabilities and a uniform density rating, Simson plywood is easy to saw, drill, and fabricate. It is ideal for internal structural paneling, packaging boxes, partition frames, wardrobe backing sheets, and residential ceiling work.

📏 Simson Plywood in the Rajkot Range

Simson is one of the branded plywood lines Mahadev Traders holds as an authorised dealer. Rajkot stock runs 6mm, 8mm, 12mm, 15mm and 18mm in MR and IS 710 grades.

The 15mm and 18mm boards are the working thicknesses for carcasses and shutters; 6mm and 8mm are backing panels and drawer bottoms. Choose IS 710 where the panel meets water and MR where it does not — the plywood range page explains how the grades differ.

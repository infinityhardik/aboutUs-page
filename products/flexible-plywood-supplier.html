📘 Comprehensive Product Overview

Flexible plywood (also known as flexi ply or bendy plywood) is a specialized grade of plywood designed specifically to bend and curve into various shapes. It is composed of thin veneers of hardwood that are bonded together with high-quality resins, allowing it to curve without cracking or splitting. With a typical bend radius as low as 5cm to 10cm depending on thickness, it is widely utilized for creating curved cabinets, round tables, reception desks, archways, and structural pillars.

📐 Technical Specifications & Standards

Available in thicknesses of 4mm, 6mm, 8mm, and 12mm, our calibrated flexible plywood guarantees uniform thickness across the entire sheet. It offers exceptional bending capabilities along both the grain (long grain bend) and across the grain (cross grain bend), allowing unmatched versatility for complex carpentry designs in Gujarat's modern homes.

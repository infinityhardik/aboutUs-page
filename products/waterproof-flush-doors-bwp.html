Why BWP Flush Doors are Essential for High-Moisture Areas

A BWP Flush Door (Boiling Water Proof) is engineered to withstand the most extreme moisture conditions. Unlike standard MR grade doors that only resist humidity, BWP doors are bonded with high-quality Phenol Formaldehyde (PF) resin, which creates a permanent waterproof bond between the core and the veneers.

At Mahadev Traders, our BWP flush doors conform to the IS:2202 (Part 1) standard. As a leading flush door supplier in Rajkot, we recommend these doors specifically for areas with high water exposure, such as bathrooms, kitchen utility areas, and balconies. These doors are tested to withstand 72 hours of continuous boiling water without any delamination, ensuring they never swell or warp even in Gujarat's humid coastal climate.

Whether you are an architect designing a luxury bungalow or a contractor working on a commercial project in Rajkot or Ahmedabad, our BWP flush doors offer the reliability and longevity your clients expect.

🔍 When to Choose BWP Grade over MR Grade?

While MR grade doors are suitable for bedrooms, BWP is a must-have for the following applications:

  • Bathrooms: Constant exposure to steam and direct water splashes makes BWP the only viable long-term choice.
  • Main Doors: If your entrance door is exposed to rain or direct sunlight, a BWP flush door with a decorative laminate is highly recommended.
  • Terrace/Balcony: Exterior doors facing the elements need the boiling-water-proof protection of IS:2202 standards.
  • Pro Tip: For complete peace of mind, pair your BWP door with 710 Marine Plywood frames.

📘 Comprehensive Product Overview

Waterproof Bathroom Doors supplied by Mahadev Traders are specially engineered to withstand constant exposure to steam, water splashes, high humidity, and direct wet conditions. Unlike standard wooden doors that swell and decay in damp conditions, these doors use high-density BWP core fillers coated with resin or PVC sheet lamination to provide an absolute barrier against moisture.

📐 Technical Specifications & Standards

These doors are chemical resistant and easy to clean, preventing the growth of mold, mildew, or fungus in wet zones. They are ideal for residential bathrooms, hospital toilets, hotel restrooms, and gym shower chambers, providing a lifetime of warp-free service.

Bathroom Door Site Supply

Bathroom doors are subject to the most aggressive interior conditions, including constant steam, direct water splashes, and high humidity fluctuations. Standard wood doors often swell, warp, or develop fungal growth in these environments. Our waterproof bathroom doors service in Rajkot offers a specialized range of Boiling Water Proof (BWP) flush doors and PVC-laminated systems designed to remain dimensionally stable even under continuous exposure to moisture.

At Mahadev Traders, we distribute doors that are manufactured using high-density hardwood cores bonded with Phenol Formaldehyde (PF) synthetic resin. This 'PF bonding' is what gives our doors their 'Waterproof' status, allowing them to pass rigorous 72-hour boiling water immersion tests. Furthermore, we supply specialized PVC-encapsulated doors that provide a 100% moisture barrier on all six sides, making them virtually immune to the effects of steam and humidity common in modern high-pressure shower zones.

From our distribution center on Gondal Road, we support large-scale residential townships and hotel renovation projects in Rajkot and Jamnagar. We provide technical consultation on the selection of door cores and surface finishes, ensuring that your project's bathroom doors remain functional and aesthetic for decades without the risk of core-rot or surface-delamination.

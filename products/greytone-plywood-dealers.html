📘 Comprehensive Product Overview

Greytone Plywood is a premium brand of calibrated commercial plywood known for its high density, outstanding core strength, and structural integrity. Made from selected hardwood veneers and bonded with high-quality resins under high temperature and pressure, it features minimal core gaps and excellent resistance to structural warping or twisting over time.

📐 Technical Specifications & Standards

It is highly recommended for residential and commercial furniture including wardrobes, beds, display racks, and panels. Our Greytone calibrated plywood is available in 6x3, 6x4, 7x3, 7x4 and 8x4 sheets in thicknesses of 6mm, 9mm, 12mm and 18mm. The wood is seasoned under vacuum pressure treatment to ensure moisture content is kept strictly between 8% and 12%.

📏 Greytone Plywood in the Rajkot Range

Greytone plywood is stocked in Rajkot in four thicknesses — 6mm, 9mm, 12mm and 18mm — in 6x3, 6x4, 7x3, 7x4 and 8x4 ft sheets, on a Gurjan face with a red-and-white alternate core. It is supplied wholesale to carpenters and furniture workshops across Rajkot, Morbi, Jamnagar and the wider Saurashtra region.

18mm is the carcass and shutter thickness; 12mm suits shelving and internal dividers; 6mm and 9mm are backing panels. Greytone is stocked as an MR (semi-waterproof) grade line, which is the right bonding for dry interior furniture. For a kitchen, bathroom or any position that meets water, ask for a BWP board instead — the plywood range page sets out how the grades differ by position.

What Is Block Board & Why Do Gujarat's Top Carpenters Prefer It?

A block board is a core-based engineered wood product where solid timber strips (battens) are edge-glued together and sandwiched between veneer layers. This construction gives block boards superior strength, rigidity, and screw-holding capacity compared to plywood of the same thickness — making them the preferred choice for heavy-duty furniture applications.

🔍 Block Board vs Plywood — When to Use Which?

This is one of the most common questions we get from carpenters and interior designers. Here is a practical guide:

ParameterBlock BoardPlywood
Best ForLarge flat surfaces: wardrobes, tables, partitions, long shelvesCurved/complex shapes, kitchen carcass, shutters
Span StrengthExcellent — no sagging even on 8-foot spansMay sag on long unsupported spans
Screw HoldingSuperior due to solid wood coreGood but weaker on edges
Bending/CurvesCannot be bent or curvedCan be bent (especially flexible plywood)
WeightLighter than same-thickness plywoodHeavier due to multiple cross-grain layers
PriceSlightly lower per sq. ft. than equivalent plywoodHigher due to more processing

Our Recommendation: Use block boards for wardrobe shutters, table tops, and partitions. Use marine plywood for kitchen carcasses and wet areas. For most Gujarat residential projects, a combination of both gives the best results.

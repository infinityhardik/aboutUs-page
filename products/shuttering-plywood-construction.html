The Backbone of High-Strength Concrete Formwork

In the construction industry, the quality of your finish depends on the quality of your mold. Shuttering Plywood, also known as Film-Faced Plywood, is a specialized densified plywood used as formwork to provide a smooth, sturdy surface for pouring concrete.

At Mahadev Traders, we understand that for a builder in Rajkot, the cost per repetition is the most critical metric. Our shuttering plywood is manufactured using selected hardwood veneers and bonded with high-quality WBP (Water Boiling Proof) phenol formaldehyde resin. This ensures that the plywood can be re-used multiple times without delamination, even in the harsh, humid, and abrasive environment of a construction site.

Whether you are casting high-rise slabs, heavy-duty columns, or intricate bridge girders, our densified sheets provide the structural integrity and mirror-smooth finish required by modern engineering standards.

🔍 Buyer's Guide: 30kg vs. 38kg Shuttering Plywood

Choosing the right weight is crucial for your project's economics:

  • 30kg/32kg Sheets: Ideal for low-rise residential buildings or small slabs where only 5-8 repetitions are needed.
  • 34kg/36kg Sheets: The standard choice for commercial projects, offering 10-15 repetitions.
  • 38kg/42kg Sheets: Ultra-densified for high-rise towers and bridges, capable of 20-30 repetitions.
  • Maintenance Tip: Always clean the surface and apply shuttering oil after each use to extend the life of the plywood.

Pine-core flush doors in Rajkot

Every flush door we keep in stock is built on a pinewood core with a Gurjan face. So if you want a pine-core door, the choice is between brand, grade and size, not core.

A pinewood batten core gives a solid door that is lighter than a hardwood core and takes hinge and lock screws well. The core decides how the door handles hardware. The bonding grade, MR or BWP, decides how it handles water.

📐 Which pine door for which opening

Bedrooms and internal doors: a 25mm or 30mm MR door (Marllex A grade or Simson B grade) is enough. Bathrooms, wash areas and kitchens: use a 30mm or 35mm Marllex BWP door; an MR door swells at the bottom when it stays wet. Main doors and tall openings: 35mm Marllex BWP, held up to 96 inches high.

Whatever the grade, seal all six faces of the door, including the top and bottom edges and every edge you trim on site, with paint, polish or laminate before or straight after hanging. An unsealed bottom edge is how most doors take on water.

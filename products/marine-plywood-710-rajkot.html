What Is Marine Plywood & Why Is It Essential for Gujarat?

Marine plywood (also called BWP plywood) is the highest grade of plywood manufactured under the Indian Standard IS:710. Unlike regular commercial plywood, marine plywood uses boiling water proof (BWP) adhesives that prevent delamination even under prolonged water exposure. This makes it the only suitable choice for kitchens, bathrooms, balconies, and outdoor furniture.

In Gujarat's climate — with intense monsoon humidity in Saurashtra and coastal moisture in cities like Jamnagar and Porbandar — using anything less than marine plywood in wet areas is a recipe for swelling, warping, and fungal damage within 2–3 years. 🏗️ Where to Use Marine Plywood in Your Gujarat Project

  • Kitchen cabinets — Below-counter carcass (19mm), wall unit carcass (12–16mm), shutters (12mm). Always BWP for any area near the sink or hob.
  • Bathroom vanities — Wash basin cabinets, mirror units, and storage racks. Use 12–19mm BWP only.
  • Balcony and terrace furniture — Outdoor seating, planters, and storage. Marine plywood handles Gujarat's monsoon rains.
  • Ground-floor interiors — In areas prone to flooding or rising damp (common in parts of Rajkot and Ahmedabad during monsoon).
  • Commercial kitchens & hospitals — High-humidity commercial spaces where hygiene and durability are critical.

For dry interior areas like bedrooms and drawing rooms, commercial MR-grade plywood offers excellent value at a lower price point.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions About Marine Plywood

The number 710 refers to the Indian Standard IS:710, which specifies the requirements for boiling water proof (BWP) plywood. Plywood certified under IS:710 has been tested to withstand boiling water immersion for 72 hours without delamination. This is the highest water-resistance grade available in Indian plywood.
We strongly advise against it, especially in Gujarat's humid climate. Commercial (MR-grade) plywood handles mild humidity but will swell and delaminate when exposed to water splashes, steam from cooking, or sink overflows. Kitchen cabinets below the countertop must always use marine plywood. For wall-mounted units away from the sink, MR-grade may work but BWP is safer.
For kitchen base unit carcasses, use 18mm or 19mm marine plywood — this provides the structural strength to support stone countertops. For wall-mounted units, 12mm is standard. Cabinet shutters (doors) should be 12mm. Back panels can be 6mm. Always use BWP grade for all kitchen components.
Marine plywood typically costs 30–50% more than commercial plywood of the same thickness. However, this premium is worth it for wet areas as replacing swollen commercial plywood within 3–5 years costs far more than the initial investment in marine plywood. Contact us for current wholesale rates on both grades.
BWR (Boiling Water Resistant) can withstand water splashes but isn't meant for immersion. BWP (Boiling Water Proof) or Marine Grade can survive 72 hours of continuous boiling without delaminating, making it 100% waterproof.
Genuine IS:710 Marine ply must carry the ISI mark with the license number (CM/L). At Mahadev Traders, we provide manufacturer test certificates for every batch to ensure absolute transparency.
IS:710 plywood of the grade we supply is treated with Glue Line Protection (GLP), which carries the treatment into every glue line rather than only the face. Borer protection is guaranteed, as is the 100% boiling-water-proof bonding. Anti-termite treatment is available on request — ask for it when you place the order, and the applicable warranty terms and conditions are confirmed in writing with your quotation.
Calibrated ply is sanded on both sides to achieve an even thickness across the entire sheet. This is critical for modular kitchen work where even a 1mm deviation can cause alignment issues with cabinets.
While you can use it everywhere for maximum longevity, we usually recommend it for 'wet zones' like kitchens and bathrooms. For dry areas like bedrooms, MR Grade or Block Board is more cost-effective.
Marine plywood can be used for shuttering but it is not cost-effective. We recommend our dedicated shuttering plywood which is specifically designed for concrete formwork with film-faced surfaces for easy release and multiple reuses. It costs significantly less than marine plywood for this purpose.

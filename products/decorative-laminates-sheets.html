🔍 Designer's Guide: Choosing the Right Texture for Each Room

Room-specific texture selection can dramatically change the vibe of your home:

  • Living Room: Use Wood-grain or Marble textures for TV units. Glossy laminates on highlight walls can make the room appear larger and brighter.
  • Master Bedroom: Soft Fabric or Suede finishes create a warm, cozy atmosphere. Pair with quality block board for heavy wardrobe shutters.
  • Kitchen: Stick to High Gloss or Anti-Fingerprint Matte for ease of cleaning. Avoid deep textures where oil and dust can accumulate.
  • Office: Solid colors in Suede finish provide a professional, glare-free look that is easy on the eyes during long working hours.

❓ Common Questions About Decorative Laminates

HPL (High-Pressure Laminate) is manufactured under much higher pressure, making it thicker (typically 1.0mm+) and more durable than LPL (Low-Pressure Laminate), which is often pre-pasted on boards. For custom furniture in Rajkot, we always recommend HPL for the best finish.
Thicker laminates (1.25mm) provide better structural strength and are more resistant to 'telegraphing' — where minor bumps in the plywood show through the laminate. They also allow for deeper, more realistic textures and higher gloss levels.
Bubbling is usually caused by trapped air or moisture. Always use high-quality heat-resistant adhesive, ensure the plywood surface is dry and dust-free, and use a professional laminate press or heavy-duty rollers to ensure a uniform bond.
Standard decorative laminates are for indoor use only. For outdoor application, we recommend specialized 'Exterior Grade' laminates that are designed to withstand direct UV rays and rain without cracking or fading.

📏 Marllex Block Board in the Rajkot Range

Marllex premium block board is stocked in Rajkot at 19mm and 25mm, in six sheet sizes — 6x3, 6x4, 7x3, 7x4, 8x3 and 8x4 ft — built as a Gurjan face on a pinewood batten core, at A grade commercial. Six sheet sizes matter on made-to-measure work, because starting from a sheet closer to the finished size cuts waste on tall wardrobe shutters and long partitions.

Block board is preferred over plywood for tall shutters and long unsupported panels: the pinewood batten core resists bending better across a long span, and holds a screw where a hollow panel would not. This is a commercial-grade board for dry interior positions — for a kitchen or vanity carcass that meets water, take the BWP waterproof block board instead.

🔍 MR Grade (IS:303) vs. BWP Grade (IS:710) — Which do you need?

Choosing the wrong grade of plywood is the most common mistake in home interiors. Here is a simple guide to help you decide:

  • Use MR Grade (Commercial): For dry areas like bedrooms, living rooms, and study rooms. It is more economical and offers excellent durability for these spaces.
  • Don't Use MR Grade: In kitchens, bathrooms, or balconies. Constant water exposure will cause MR plywood to swell over time.
  • Recommendation: Use IS:710 Marine Plywood for kitchen carcasses and MR grade for everything else to optimize your project budget.

📘 Comprehensive Product Overview

Limelite Plywood is a trusted name for calibrated commercial and waterproof plywood in Rajkot and Gujarat. Combining advanced German machinery calibration with handpicked core veneers, Limelite offers an exceptionally flat and smooth surface that is ideal for applying thin veneers, decorative laminates (sunmica), or high-gloss paint finishes without any surface waviness.

📐 Technical Specifications & Standards

It is bonded with specialized melamine-fortified synthetic resins, yielding excellent bond strength and resistance to moisture. Its exceptional screw-holding and nail-holding capacity makes it the go-to choice for contract carpenters and modular kitchen manufacturers across Saurashtra.

📏 Limelite Plywood in the Rajkot Range

Limelite plywood is stocked in Rajkot in 6mm, 9mm, 12mm and 18mm, across 6x3, 6x4, 7x3, 7x4, 8x3 and 8x4 ft sheets. The range runs in two constructions: Limelite on a Gurjan face with a hardwood mixed core, and Limelite Pro on an Okuma face with a red-and-white alternate core.

Both are stocked as MR (semi-waterproof) grade. MR bonding is the right specification for dry interior furniture — wardrobes, shelving, partitions and cabinet carcasses in bedrooms and living rooms. It is not the grade for a kitchen sink run or a bathroom: an MR board under a sink will fail. For those positions ask for a BWP line instead, which the plywood range page compares side by side.

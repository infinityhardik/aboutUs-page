The Gold Standard of Hardwood: Understanding Gurjan Plywood

In the world of premium woodworking, Gurjan plywood stands as the undisputed benchmark for structural integrity and longevity. Derived from the Dipterocarpus species, Gurjan is a naturally dense and stable hardwood that provides the necessary 'foundation' for high-end interior projects. Our Gurjan plywood dealer service in Rajkot supplies a full Gurjan face veneer over a graded core — hardwood mixed, red-and-white alternate, or a 15-ply construction in the heavier boards. It is the face that carries the Gurjan grain, hardness and finish; ask which core suits the job when you request rates, because the core is what decides the panel's weight and screw holding.

At Mahadev Traders, we understand that a luxury wardrobe or a bespoke kitchen island is only as good as the substrate it is built upon. We distribute Gurjan plywood characterized by its heavy weight (exceeding 800 kg/m³) and its distinctive reddish-brown core. Unlike plantation timbers, Gurjan has a naturally high resistance to moisture and fungal decay, which is further enhanced by our stock's 'PF' (Phenol Formaldehyde) bonding—making it suitable even for coastal environments in Jamnagar and Dwarka.

For IS:303 commercial plywood selection, buyers can compare face quality, glue type and thickness availability before finalising a batch. ❓ Gurjan Plywood — Technical FAQs

Authentic Gurjan plywood features a consistent reddish-brown core across all layers and is significantly heavier than commercial alternatives due to its high natural density.
The high fiber density of Gurjan timber ensures superior screw-holding capacity, preventing the mechanical failure of hinges and heavy-duty drawer slides over long-term use.
Gurjan's natural oils resist pests, and treatment is applied during the veneer bonding process. Borer protection is guaranteed on the stock we supply. Anti-termite treatment is available on request — ask for it when you place the order, and the applicable warranty terms and conditions are confirmed in writing with your quotation.
Our factory-calibrated Gurjan sheets are ready for direct application of stains or polishes after standard surface cleaning, thanks to the wood's naturally smooth and tight grain structure.

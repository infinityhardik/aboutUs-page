⚡ What is Motherwood TurBo+ Grade MDF — The Highest Density MDF?

Motherwood TurBo+ Grade MDF is the highest density MDF available — sitting at the very top of the MDF quality hierarchy. The correct grade ladder from lowest to highest is: Interior Grade → Exterior Grade → Super HD+WR (HDHMR) → TurBo+ / Boilo (BEST). TurBo+ delivers the best moisture resistance and product performance of all MDF grades.

TurBo+ is the preferred choice for any application requiring maximum density, superior moisture resistance, and the best possible MDF quality — from premium modular kitchens and high-humidity environments to precision furniture and demanding commercial applications. Its ultra-smooth surface delivers exceptional results with paint, laminates, and veneers.

Gurjan, commonly associated with Dipterocarpus hardwood species from Myanmar and Southeast Asia, is a dense tropical hardwood used widely in premium plywood and block board because it offers a harder surface, tighter grain, and better resistance to everyday wear than budget face veneers. With a density signal often above 600 kg/m3 in hardwood-face applications, it is preferred where surface strength and fastener grip matter. For builders and furniture factories in Rajkot, Ahmedabad, Morbi, Jamnagar, and wider Gujarat, this makes Gurjan block board a practical choice for doors, shutters, long shelves, counters, and commercial interiors where the finished panel must stay flat after lamination or veneer pressing.

Mahadev Traders helps buyers choose the correct board by matching the core, thickness, face quality, and grade to the application. A 19mm or 25mm board is usually selected for wardrobes, modular furniture, office partitions, and storage shutters, while 30mm boards are preferred when the panel carries heavier hardware or needs a more solid feel. For humid zones or kitchen-adjacent use, we recommend confirming the bonding grade, choosing BWR or BWP where required, and using proper edge sealing so the board performs as expected over the long term. For heavy-duty furniture and structural shutters, ask for zero-void core construction and inspect the edge before bulk purchase.

How to Choose Gurjan Block Board for a Project

For premium furniture, the key selection points are face quality, core regularity, bonding grade, and sheet thickness. A clean Gurjan face helps laminates and veneers settle smoothly without visible patches. A calibrated core keeps long shutters straight and reduces vibration when heavy handles, hinges, or sliding hardware are installed. Contractors should also check the panel edges for voids before cutting, because edge quality affects screw holding and final finish.

When comparing Gurjan block board price in Rajkot, avoid judging only by per-sheet rate. A cheaper board can increase wastage if the face has patches, the thickness varies, or the core contains gaps.

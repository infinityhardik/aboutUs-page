📖 Plywood Buying Guide — What Every Buyer Should Know

Choosing the wrong plywood can lead to warping, swelling, or premature failure. Here is what we recommend based on our 30+ years of experience:

  • Always check the ISI mark — Genuine marine plywood carries the IS:710 stamp and commercial plywood carries IS:303. Ask your dealer to show the certification.
  • Match thickness to application — 19mm for carcass work, 12mm for shutters and partitions, 6mm for back panels and liners. Using 19mm everywhere wastes money; using 6mm for shelves causes sagging.
  • Consider the face veneer — Gurjan face plywood gives a smoother finish ideal for laminate pasting. Poplar and eucalyptus faces are more budget-friendly for concealed areas.
  • Buy from authorised dealers only — Counterfeit plywood is a serious issue in Gujarat. At Mahadev Traders, every sheet is sourced directly from the manufacturer with full traceability.
  • Bulk orders save significantly — If you are a contractor or manufacturer, our wholesale rates on 50+ sheets are substantially lower than retail. Call us for a custom quotation.

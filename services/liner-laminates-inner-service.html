Professional Inner Surfacing for Durable Cabinetry

While the external laminate defines the style of your furniture, the internal liner ensures its longevity and hygiene. Our liner laminates inner service in Rajkot provides the essential surfacing required to protect cabinet carcasses from moisture, dust, and termites while maintaining a clean, uniform aesthetic.

At Mahadev Traders, we supply 'balancing' laminates specifically engineered for interior surfaces. These 0.6mm to 0.8mm sheets are more than just a cost-saving measure; they prevent the base substrate (Plywood or MDF) from warping by providing equal tension to the 1mm external decorative sheet. Our inventory features high-quality white, off-white, and sophisticated fabric-textured liners that are easy to clean and resistant to the yellowing typically seen in lower-grade products.

Operating from Gondal Road, we support Rajkot's growing modular furniture industry with bulk supply chains, ensuring that contractors and OEMs have access to consistent color batches for large-scale residential and commercial fit-outs.

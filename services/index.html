📋 Grade & Specification Advice Before You Order

The most expensive mistake in panel buying is specifying by thickness and price alone. A sink cabinet built from an interior-grade board fails within a season; a dry wardrobe built from BWP costs more than it needed to. Send us the room, the moisture exposure, the hardware load and the finish, and we will tell you which grade the job actually needs — including when a cheaper board is the correct answer.

Batch-Matched Bulk Supply

For pressed-laminate runs and matching furniture units we hold sheets from one batch together. A density or shade change mid-run shows up in the finished work, so batch consistency is reserved at order time rather than hoped for at dispatch.

Just-in-Time Factory Supply

Modular kitchen and furniture manufacturers can schedule staged deliveries instead of warehousing a full order. We hold the stock in Rajkot and release it against your production plan.

Tender & Compliance Documents

For project tenders we can issue the full documentation set for the boards supplied — moisture-content certificates, IS grade declarations and dealership letters. Ask for what the tender requires when you place the order and it will be prepared with the consignment.

Formwork Repeat-Cycle Planning

Shuttering plywood is bought on cycles, not sheets. We work back from the number of pours and the finish required to a board weight and film grade that survives the cycle count.

Catalogue & Shade Selection

Laminate and sunmica selection happens against physical samples and current catalogues, with shade availability checked before you commit a full interior scheme to a code.

Site & Workshop Delivery

Scheduled dispatch from the Gondal Road warehouse to sites and workshops across Rajkot, Morbi, Jamnagar, Ahmedabad and the wider Saurashtra region.

📦 Minimum Order & How We Quote

There is no minimum order quantity. A single sheet or door is a perfectly normal order. That matters on a project: you can take one piece of a grade you have not used before, test it against your own work, and only then place the bulk order.

Rates are quoted per enquiry. Price depends on brand, thickness, grade, the batch in stock, the quantity and the delivery destination, so we quote against your actual job rather than publishing a list. Send the room, grade, thickness, quantity and delivery point and the rate comes back together with the grade recommendation.

Delivery across Gujarat, freight quoted with the order. Freight is charged per order and varies with the region, the service available on that route and the timing, so it is quoted alongside the material rate rather than fixed in advance. Free delivery is available on select orders — worth asking when you request the rate, because it can change which quantity makes sense to take in one go.

Where We Recommend Poplar, and Where We Do Not

Poplar block board is a sensible choice for wardrobes, lofts, low-load partitions, side panels, and budget-sensitive residential furniture. We do not recommend it as the first choice for high-load bed frames, heavy shutters, wet kitchens, or areas where a pine or BWP board will perform better over time.

❓ Block Board Service — FAQs

Choose block board for large unsupported surfaces like wardrobe shutters or office partitions. It is much more resistant to bending (warping) over long spans compared to plywood, which is better for structural "skeletons."
Pine core boards are heavier, denser, and have superior screw-holding capacity, making them ideal for high-end furniture. Poplar core boards are lighter and more cost-effective, suitable for budget modular furniture.
For bulk factory orders, we can facilitate custom-sized boards directly from our manufacturing partners. For regular retail/contractor orders, we supply standard 8x4 sheets from our ready stock.
Authentic BWP block boards will have a specific IS:1659 marking. We also provide a 72-hour boiling water test certificate from the manufacturer for bulk institutional supplies.
We coordinate next-day delivery for Ahmedabad and Saurashtra regions for orders placed before 1 PM. Our logistics team ensures damage-free unloading at the site.

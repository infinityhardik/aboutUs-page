❓ Sunmica & Laminate Service — FAQs

1mm laminates are thicker, more durable, and offer better resistance to chipping and warping, making them ideal for external surfaces. 0.8mm sheets are more economical and are typically used for residential wardrobes or areas with moderate use.
Yes. For our primary wholesale brands, we can coordinate matching PVC edge-bands (0.8mm or 2mm) to ensure a seamless factory finish for modular furniture manufacturers.
Liner laminates (usually 0.6mm or 0.7mm) are applied to the inside of cabinets and drawers. They provide a clean, moisture-resistant finish and help balance the board to prevent warping.
For high-gloss sheets, we recommend specialized adhesives like Fevicol Heat-X or equivalent industrial-grade contact cements. These ensure a flatter bond and minimize the 'orange peel' effect common with standard adhesives.
Bulk sheets are stacked face-to-face with protective inter-leaving. We use dedicated transport vehicles with flat beds to ensure that sheets remain perfectly flat and crack-free during transit across Gujarat.

### Marllex Plywood in the Rajkot Range

Marllex is one of the branded plywood lines Mahadev Traders stocks as an authorised dealer, alongside Zevik and Simson. Branded stock matters most on projects where sheets are pressed with laminate or cut on a machine, because a batch that varies in thickness or density shows up in the finished unit.

The Marllex premium plywood held in Rajkot runs 6mm, 9mm, 12mm, 16mm, 19mm and 25mm in 8x4 ft sheets at IS 710 grade. IS 710 is the boiling-water-proof specification, so this line suits kitchen carcasses, sink runs, bathroom vanities and any position where the panel will meet water, as well as ordinary dry furniture.

### Choosing a Thickness

For wardrobe and cabinet carcasses 19mm is the usual choice; 12mm and 16mm suit shelving and drawer sides; 6mm and 9mm are backing and panel infill. 25mm is for worktops and heavy shutters that carry their own hardware load.

If the panel is going into a dry bedroom or living room and budget is tight, an MR grade board will do the same job for less - see the commercial MR plywood page. Paying for IS 710 makes sense where water is genuinely a risk.

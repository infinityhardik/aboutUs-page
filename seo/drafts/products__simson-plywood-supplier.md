### Simson Plywood in the Rajkot Range

Simson is one of the branded plywood lines Mahadev Traders holds as an authorised dealer. Rajkot stock runs 6mm, 8mm, 12mm, 15mm and 18mm in MR and IS 710 grades.

The 15mm and 18mm boards are the working thicknesses for carcasses and shutters; 6mm and 8mm are backing panels and drawer bottoms. Choose IS 710 where the panel meets water and MR where it does not.

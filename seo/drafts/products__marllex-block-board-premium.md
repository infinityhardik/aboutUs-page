### Marllex Block Board in the Rajkot Range

Marllex premium block board is stocked in Rajkot at 25mm and 30mm in 6x3, 6x4, 7x4 and 8x4 ft sheets, at BWP grade. The choice of four sheet sizes matters on made-to-measure work, because starting from a sheet closer to the finished size cuts waste on tall wardrobe shutters and long partitions.

Block board is preferred over plywood for tall shutters and long unsupported panels: the batten core resists bending better across a long span. At BWP grade this board also suits kitchen and vanity carcasses where a standard MR block board would swell.

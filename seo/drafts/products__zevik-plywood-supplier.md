### Zevik Plywood in the Rajkot Range

Zevik is one of the branded plywood lines Mahadev Traders holds as an authorised dealer. The Rajkot stock covers MR, BWP and IS 710 grades, so the same brand can serve both dry interior furniture and wet-area work without changing supplier mid-project.

Grade is the decision that matters here, not brand. MR is bonded for dry interiors; BWP and IS 710 are boiling-water-proof and belong in kitchens, bathrooms and anywhere exposed to standing water or steam.

### Where Exterior Grade Sits in the Motherwood Range

Motherwood Exterior Grade MDF is bonded for semi-outdoor and humid positions: covered ceilings, balcony soffits, shop fit-out panels and interiors that stay damp. Rajkot stock runs 3mm, 5.5mm, 7.3mm, 11mm and 17mm in 8x4 ft sheets.

"Exterior grade" describes the bonding, not an ability to survive weather unprotected. It is not a substitute for marine plywood in a position exposed to direct rain or standing water. Seal the back face and all four edges - on exterior grade this matters more than on any interior board, because an unsealed edge is where moisture enters and the panel starts to swell.

### Where Super HD+WR Sits in the Motherwood Range

Super HD+WR is Motherwood's HDHMR grade - high density plus water resistance - and it is the board to specify for the sink run, the bathroom vanity and tall wardrobes in humid rooms. It resists swelling far better than standard MDF, and its density gives better screw holding for the hinges and runners modular furniture depends on.

Rajkot stock covers seven thicknesses: 3mm, 5.5mm, 7.5mm, 11mm, 16.75mm, 18mm and 25mm, all in 8x4 ft sheets. 18mm is the usual carcass thickness and 25mm suits worktops and heavy shutters.

Water resistance is not the same as waterproof. Every cut edge needs sealing before the unit is installed, and a position taking direct rain or standing water calls for marine plywood, not MDF of any grade.

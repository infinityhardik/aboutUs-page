### Limelite Plywood in the Rajkot Range

Limelite plywood is stocked in Rajkot in MR and BWP grades. MR bonding is for dry interior furniture - wardrobes, shelving, partitions and cabinet carcasses in bedrooms and living rooms. BWP is the boiling-water-proof grade for kitchen and bathroom positions.

As with every plywood line, specify by the room and the moisture exposure first and by brand second. A BWP board in a dry bedroom is money spent on performance the furniture will never need; an MR board under a sink will fail.

### Greytone Plywood in the Rajkot Range

Greytone plywood is stocked in Rajkot from 6mm to 19mm in 8x4 ft sheets, supplied wholesale to carpenters and furniture workshops across Rajkot, Morbi, Jamnagar and the wider Saurashtra region.

19mm is the carcass and shutter thickness; 12mm and 16mm suit shelving and internal dividers; 6mm and 9mm are backing panels. Before ordering, confirm which grade the job needs - see the plywood range page for how MR, BWR and IS 710 bonding differ by position.

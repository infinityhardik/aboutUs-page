### Marllex Flush Doors in the Rajkot Range

Marllex premium flush doors are stocked in Rajkot at 30mm, 32mm, 35mm and 38mm, manufactured to IS 2202 at BWP grade. IS 2202 is the Indian Standard for wooden flush door shutters, and BWP bonding means the door core is built for positions that meet moisture.

Thickness is chosen by where the door hangs and what hardware it carries. 30mm and 32mm are typical internal room doors; 35mm and 38mm suit main doors, taller openings and doors carrying heavier locksets or closers. Every cut edge and the bottom rail should be sealed before hanging, whatever the grade.

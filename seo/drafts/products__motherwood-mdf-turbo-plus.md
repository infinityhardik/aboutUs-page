### Where TurBo+ Sits in the Motherwood Range

TurBo+ is the densest grade Motherwood makes, and the reason to specify it is machining behaviour rather than moisture resistance. Routed jali panels, profiled shutter edges and CNC detail come off the machine with less edge chipping on a dense core, which cuts finishing time on high-value work.

Rajkot stock runs 3mm, 5.5mm, 8mm, 12mm and 18mm in 8x4 ft sheets. If the panel is going somewhere humid, Super HD+WR is the grade to ask for instead - density and moisture resistance are different properties, and TurBo+ is chosen for the first.
